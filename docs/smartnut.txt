SmartNUT 
========

Nowadays, smart setups already includes the necessary communication bus (MQTT or any smart protocol), and protection means (automations).
These smart setup only consider the smarter units (USB, even cheap, and networked units like SNMP and XML-PDC).
As a result, many NUT limitations can be removed, to focus on drivers and event based mechanism.

SmartNUT is a refreshed form-factor of NUT suited to modern integrations, like in Home Assistant or any smart setup.
It removes the need for the server (upsd) and client (upsmon, HA Integration) layers, by connecting the drivers to a
new and selectable communication bus (MQTT, HomeKit, ...). It also limits the drivers set to USB and networked (SNMP
and NetXML-UPS), which are more suited to modern setups.

As a result, devices supported by NUT will be exposed as MQTT, HomeKit or any other smart device, using a communication backend implemented.
NUT UPS will thus becomes smart-things.
